---
name: Artist browsing
description: Why artist discovery uses a list instead of a decision deck
---

Artist discovery should let people browse artists independently of reservation and saving. Photo swipes should only change photos.

**Why:** Removing the pass action and decision swipes would leave a one-at-a-time deck without a way to reach other artists. A scrollable list preserves access to everyone while saving remains non-destructive.

**How to apply:** When changing the artist browsing experience, do not make saving, reserving, or horizontal card gestures necessary to reach the next artist.
